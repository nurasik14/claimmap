# ClaimMap pitch cue card (glance at this, don't read it)

## Before you go up
- VPN on. App open in full screen (F11). Run `bash scripts/reset_demo.sh` after your LAST rehearsal.
- Warm up live mode: press Replay once, then stop it. Sound up, tested on the room's speakers.
- Open the LabLoop (deck) card. Keep the recorded backup video in a second window.

## The 7 points you must not miss
1. **The problem.** A deck makes dozens of claims, but the deal rests on 3 or 4. If one of those is
   false, the deal falls. VCs spend 118 hours of due diligence per deal (Gompers et al., survey of 885 VCs).
2. **Name the competitors first.** GoPresent and TechTruth check deck claims one by one. Faktum and
   VeriFact fact-check spoken sentences. Our difference: what the deal rests on, and the founder
   versus their own deck, live.
3. **The deck demo (Read tab).** "A sample deck WE WROTE." "No direct competitors" turns red, with
   real companies and source links: Metrohm, Labman, Curiox.
4. **The map demo (Map tab).** Bigger circle = more of the deal rests on it. The diamonds are the
   evidence: green proof, red refutation. Hover "26 paying customers", press P:
   **6 of 16 claims fall, including the ask.** "That's the question you open the meeting with."
5. **The live demo (Live tab).** Replay with the sound on. The deck says 26 customers, the founder
   says 40, and it's caught about 4 seconds later (measured: median 4.1 s, max 5.7 s), along with
   $4.1B vs "over $10B". The score drops live and the question is already written.
6. **The Brief.** A one-page memo for your partners: recommendation, red flags linked to claims,
   what changed during the pitch, documents to request. Export in one click.
7. **The ask.** "2 or 3 funds to pilot it on their next 10 decks. Talk to us after."

## Strong true lines (use 1 or 2)
- "We ran ClaimMap on our own pitch deck. It caught us: we'd written that nobody checks pitches
  live, and it found three tools that do. We fixed the slide."
- "It won't turn a claim green without an independent source, and it shows the quote for every red flag."
- "The score is grounded in published research, and it shows which claims moved it."
- "Built today. Tested on 3 real founder decks and 8 TechCrunch Battlefield final pitches."

## Numbers you may say (all real)
- 118 hours of due diligence, survey of 885 VCs (Gompers et al. 2020)
- 6 of 16 claims fall when "26 customers" is pulled, including the ask
- Live alert about 4 s after the sentence (median 4.1 s, max 5.7 s, on our demo pitch)
- Deck analysis takes about 25-90 s; speech-to-text runs locally, 30-60x faster than real time
- 3 real decks and 8 real Battlefield pitches processed end to end
- TechTruth charges VC funds EUR 799 for a beta trial (on their website), so funds pay for this

## Never say
- That LabLoop is a real company. It's "a sample deck we wrote".
- Any accuracy %, cost per deck or customer number. We have none yet: "that's what the pilot measures".
- "No competitors" or "nobody does this live". Our own tool proved that wrong.
- That the score predicts winners. It doesn't (we tested it), and it doesn't judge the team.
- "GoPresent / TechTruth only give flat lists." ClaimMap flagged that line as contradicted.

## If they ask...
- **Accuracy?** "Not measured at scale yet. On a deck with planted problems it caught every one; on
  real decks it found things we didn't know, including on our own. The pilot measures it properly."
- **AI is wrong?** "Every flag has its source and quote. Unverified isn't false; it becomes a
  question. The arithmetic checks are plain code."
- **Founders hate it?** "The founder never sees it. The investor does, on their laptop."
- **Privacy?** "Runs locally in this build. Speech-to-text runs on this laptop. We don't train on decks."
- **Moat?** "Claim memory: next round, we know what the founder claimed last time and whether they hit it."
- **Business model?** "Per seat for funds, a free tier for angels."
- **Does the score pick winners?** "No. We tested it on 8 Battlefield finals and it doesn't, and we say
  so. It measures whether claims hold up, which is what diligence needs."
- **What's next?** "Pick any fund's or competition's rubric and score the pitch against it, with
  evidence for every mark."

## If something breaks
- Live replay is silent or slow: type "We have 40 paying customers" in the Live tab and press Enter.
- The app is down: play the recorded backup video: "Here's the recorded run."
- Never wait in silence: keep talking over the delay.
